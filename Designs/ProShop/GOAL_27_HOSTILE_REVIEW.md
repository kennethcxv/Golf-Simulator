# GOAL 27 — HOSTILE REVIEW OF EVERY SHIPPED ASSET

Reviewed from the frames only, at full size, one frame at a time. No re-renders,
no fixes. Faults are cited by frame number. Where a fault was too small to be
certain at full size, it was cropped and magnified 4-7x and the crop is named.

**Three "evidence" frames in this set are blank grey images** — `dustpan-under.png`,
`rake-under.png`, `mower-reel.png`. The camera photographed nothing. Every claim
that rests on those three views is void, and one of them (`mower-reel`) is the
only frame that was supposed to show a greens mower's reel.

---

## 1. THE HAND — ITERATE

Frames viewed: tt00–tt07 (all eight), silhouette, apparent, vs-reference.

- **tt03** — an open **hole** through the surface between the index knuckle and
  the finger, ~35x95 px in a 760 px frame, showing lit brown backfaces inside.
  Not a shadow: the interior is lit. (crop 470,250 220x180 @4x)
- **tt02** — a second break at the same knuckle band: a hard facet with an orange
  backface sliver at the surface edge. The surface folds and does not close.
- **tt06, tt07** — the same orange speck recurs from two more angles.
- **silhouette** — two white specks *inside* the black mass, at approx (250,410)
  and (460,510). Daylight through the mesh. This is the check that should have
  caught it and it was printed and not looked at.
- **tt00** — from the shaft's end the hand is a featureless ball. No finger
  separation at all.
- **tt04** — fingertips hang in front of and below the shaft without closing on
  it. Nothing grips.
- **tt05, tt07** — the forearm is a club: a bowling-pin mass roughly twice the
  hand, ending in a blunt round stump with no elbow and no wrist structure.
- **vs-reference** — against `HandsRefrenceImage.png`: fingers are uniform
  sausages, no nails, no knuckle row, no tendons. The thumb reads as a separate
  lobe lying along the shaft rather than opposing it.

## 2. THE MOP HEAD — ITERATE (and it is the worst item here)

**There is no turntable for this asset.** It was never brought through the hero
pipeline; it was parameter-tuned by bisection. Reviewed from the shipped tuning
point `mop-bisect-c-splay0.95-len0.108.png`, `mop-vs-reference.png` and
`held-mop.png`.

- **mop-bisect-c** (crop 1120,940 420x340 @3x) — the strand ring is **still
  detached from the red hub**, with clear background visible in the gap, and it
  is offset sideways by roughly a third of a hub diameter. This is the exact
  fault the brief named as unresolved. It is not fixed.
- Same frame — the head is **hollow**: you see straight through the middle of the
  mop to the floor.
- Same frame — the **shaft passes through the red hub** and its tip is visible
  inside a black opening in the hub's top face.
- Same frame — the strands are flat ribbons with hard hooked ends; they read as
  bent paperclips. The reference is dense yarn.
- The hub is a flat disc with a white outline; it reads as a printed sticker.

## 3. THE DUSTPAN — ITERATE

## 5. THE CLOTH AND SPONGE — ITERATE

Frames viewed: tt00–tt07 (all eight), low, above, apparent, silhouette.

  of the fold's end. The "folded cloth" is two plates with an unclosed end.

## 8. THE LEDGER BOOK — ITERATE

## 10. THE MONEY — ITERATE (softest of the list)

Frames viewed: hero, cards, edge, tt02, plus the eevee set.

- **all frames** — every note is the **same mint green at the same size**. Only the
  word and the numeral change. A stack still reads as photocopied, which is the
  thing the spec set out to prevent.
- **edge** — the "edge" proof is a low three-quarter view, never edge-on. The
  claim that the card now has real thickness is not demonstrated by the frame
  taken to demonstrate it.
- **hero, tt02** — the coins are four near-featureless discs; three are the same
  pale grey. No relief, no milling. Sizes do differ, which is right.
- **turntable** — the money "turntable" rotates a flat lay. No item is ever seen in
  the round, so the set has no turntable in the sense the other assets have one.
- The twelve card faces are genuinely good and read as a wallet's worth of cards.

# THE TWO STANDING QUESTIONS

**Does every part connect to what it grows out of?**
No, in eleven of the twelve named assets. Counted: the mop's strand ring (loose
from the hub), the dustpan's two side fins and its handle stem, the spray's
trigger flap and dip tube, the bag's two handles (through the rim), the rake's
orphan tine and butt-joined shaft, the ledger's two page blocks and five spine
bands, the register's two stand brackets and two monitor slabs, the basket's two
bail legs and orphan stud pair, the wand's trigger blade, the mower's handlebar
rod, the spreader's frame bar, the rack's short shelves and floating upright.

**Is anything inside anything it should not be?**
Yes: the wand's barrel through its blue shell (unchanged from your report), the
mop's shaft through the hub, the bag's handles through the rim, the ledger's
bands through the cover board, the basket's handle legs through the wall and the
two bails through each other, the spray's collar into the bottle shoulder, the
dustpan's walls through the pan floor, the merch cans through each other, the
spreader's wheel into the hopper.

**Open holes, seen as daylight or backfaces:** the hand (2, visible in the
silhouette), the wand's grip, the cloth's fold end, the register's divider bases,
the merch cans' tops, the softgoods caps.

# NOTHING SHIPS

Twelve named assets, zero SHIP. The closed ledger and the hose nozzle are the two
that are close; the bag is third. The mop and the wand are the two that should
never have been reported done.
