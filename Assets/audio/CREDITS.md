# Audio credits

Every recorded sample shipped with GOLF EMPIRE, with its licence and where it
came from. **Generated by `tools/audio/write-credits.mjs` from
`Assets/audio/manifest.json` — do not hand-edit.** The test suite gates it:
a sample without a licence and a source cannot ship, and a sample in the
manifest that is missing here fails `tests/audio-sample-licences.test.js`.

**68 files across 26 cues.** Every one is CC0 or CC-BY;
anything carrying a NonCommercial or ShareAlike term was refused at download
time by `tools/audio/freesound-fetch.mjs`, which re-checks the licence on each
sound's own page rather than trusting the search filter.
