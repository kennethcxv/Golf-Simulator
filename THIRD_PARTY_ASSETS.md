## Licence policy

GOLF EMPIRE ships on Steam, so only licences that are **free for commercial use
with no attribution trap and no share-alike obligation** are accepted:
**CC0-1.0**, **CC-BY-3.0**, **CC-BY-4.0**. NonCommercial, ShareAlike and
Sampling+ terms are refused at download time, not at review time.

The shipped game plays **vendored local files only** — there is no runtime call
to any remote service. Downloads happen at development time through
`tools/audio/freesound-fetch.mjs`.
