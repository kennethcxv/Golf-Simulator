## Verification

- Card route: incomplete swipe refused; first-card decline/retry path exercised in
  the accepted run; approval, receipt, bagging, and handoff completed through normal
  controls. Final revenue `$66`, units `2`, order-held UIDs `0`, order transaction
  inactive, carrier visible.
- Cash route: `$80` tender accepted, drawer opened, three notes deposited, exact
  `$14` change counted and handed back, then receipt/bag/handoff completed. Final
  revenue `$66`, units `2`, order-held UIDs `0`, order transaction inactive,
  carrier visible.
- Browser/page error count: `0` on both accepted routes.
- Runner diagnostics contain only the already-recorded Canvas readback warning,
  Chromium shader warning, and bootstrap-navigation `ERR_ABORTED` asset requests;
  no 404, page exception, or failed transaction request occurred. These warnings
  remain explicit iteration-3 work.
- Pure guidance suite covers printer-ready gating and the in-motion handoff state.
- Blender 5.1.2 rebuilt only `bag_closed.glb`; raw Tripo assets were not touched.

## Weaknesses carried into iteration 3

1. Customer body and face still read as low-detail primitives.
2. The cashier has no visible first-person hands during grabs or swipes.
3. The physical card is not visibly pinched by a cashier hand at the reader.
4. Cash deposit/change movement has no player wrist or finger silhouette.
5. Receipt take motion lacks a visible pinch/tear gesture.
6. The customer-facing reader display remains weak from the cashier view.
7. Old low-priority toasts can remain after their transaction stage changes.
8. Printer/card/drawer audio lacks a dedicated spatial mix and print-motor loop.
9. A newly arriving ambient shopper can crowd the completion framing.
10. Repeated Canvas readback and Chromium shader warnings remain in diagnostics.

The formal three-run after-performance comparison remains scheduled after the
fourth visual revision so it measures the complete checkout presentation against
the recorded baseline under identical conditions.
